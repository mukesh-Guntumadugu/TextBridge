# TextBridge SMS: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

TextBridge is a student hackathon project (Hackpalachia, Group 3): a text line that connects
people in Southeast Ohio with community health workers and helps them book clinic visits.

## What we collect

- Your mobile phone number and the text messages you send us.
- What you tell us to find care: your ZIP code or town, and whether you have low income,
  Medicaid or no insurance.
- If you book at a clinic that uses an electronic health record, your first and last name and
  date of birth, so the clinic can find or create your patient record.

## How we use it

Only to answer your messages, connect you with a community health worker, book, change or
cancel visits, and send you a reminder before a visit.

## Who we share it with

- **The clinic you book with**, through its scheduling system (for example athenahealth), so
  your visit can be booked.
- **Our text message provider** (Twilio), only to deliver messages.
- **Community health workers** working with TextBridge, so they can help you.

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or
promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with
any third parties.** We never sell your information.

## Your choices

- Reply **STOP** to stop all messages.
- To have your messages and information deleted, reply by text asking us to delete your data,
  or open an issue at https://github.com/mukesh-Guntumadugu/TextBridge/issues (do not post
  personal details there).

## Security and retention

Data is stored on the TextBridge server and used only for the purposes above. As a hackathon
prototype, TextBridge keeps data only for the length of the project and deletes it afterwards.
