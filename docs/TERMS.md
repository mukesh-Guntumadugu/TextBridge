# TextBridge SMS: Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 14, 2026

TextBridge ("we") is a text message service, built as a student hackathon project (Hackpalachia,
Group 3), that connects people in Southeast Ohio with community health workers and helps them
book clinic visits by text.

1. **Program.** When you text the TextBridge number, we reply to help with your request: finding
   and booking a clinic visit, or passing your message to a community health worker. You may
   also receive booking confirmations and one reminder 24 hours before a booked visit.
2. **Opting in.** You opt in by texting the TextBridge number first. We never text a number that
   has not texted us.
3. **Message frequency** varies with your conversation. Message and data rates may apply.
4. **Opting out.** Reply **STOP** at any time to stop all messages, including reminders. Reply
   **START** to opt back in.
5. **Help.** Reply **HELP** for help.
6. **Not for emergencies.** TextBridge is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger,
   call **911**. For a mental health crisis, call or text **988**.
7. **Not medical advice.** Messages from TextBridge are not medical advice. Clinic details and
   times are provided by the clinics and may change; call the clinic to confirm.
8. **Delivery.** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
9. **Changes.** We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version.
10. **Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mukesh-Guntumadugu/TextBridge/issues
    or reply HELP by text.

See also our Privacy Policy: https://github.com/mukesh-Guntumadugu/TextBridge/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md
